Our quotations

Everything is listed

Our quotation shows each item with the quantity and price, so you know what you are paying for. It usually covers:

  • Roofing sheets (thickness and metres)
  • Ridge cap, barge board, eaves angle
  • Fascia and ceiling cladding
  • Wall flashing and trimming
  • Drive screws, seam bolts, rivets
  • Silicone and flash band
  • Wood work, where needed
  • Transport and workmanship
