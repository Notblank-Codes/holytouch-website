Our quotations

Everything is listed

Our quotation shows each item with the quantity and price, so you know what you are paying for. It usually covers:

  • Roofing sheets (thickness and metres)
  • Ridge cap, barge board, eaves angle
  • Fascia and ceiling cladding
  • Wall flashing and trimming
  • Drive screws, seam bolts, rivets
  • Silicone and flash band
  • Wood work, where needed
  • Transport and workmanship
How it works

From your first call to the finished roof

  1. Call or WhatsApp us

    Tell us about the building. You can send your plan or pictures.

  2. We come and measure

    We check the walls and the wood, and talk through the sheet type and colour.

  3. You get the quotation

    Every material, transport and workmanship, item by item.

  4. We fix the roof

    Our team does the work, clears the site and hands over to you.
