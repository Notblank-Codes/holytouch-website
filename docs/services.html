Services

What we do

We are roofers. Below is the work we take on. If you are not sure what your building needs, send us pictures and we will tell you.

01

New roofing

Supply and installation for new buildings: houses, duplexes, schools, shops, warehouses and other buildings.

We fix the three main types of aluminium roofing used in Nigeria:

  • Longspan: one sheet runs from the edge of the roof to the top, so there are fewer joints.
  • Step tile: aluminium pressed in steps so it looks like tiles.
  • Stone coated: metal tiles with a stone finish (Gerard and similar). Quieter when it rains.

Most people go for 0.45mm or 0.55mm, but we can fix your preferred thickness, in different colours. See the materials page for the differences.

02

Installation only

Already bought your roofing sheets and accessories? We can come and fix them.

We work as roofers for house owners, builders and contractors. You provide the materials, and we bring the team and tools and do the installation. We will check what you have bought and tell you before we start if anything is short.

Book installation
03

Re-roofing

For old roofs that are rusted, sagging or leaking in many places.

We remove the old sheets, check the wood and change any that is rotten or eaten by termites, then fix the new roof. We take the old sheets away when we finish.

Ask about re-roofing
04

Leak repairs

The place where water drips is often not where it is coming in.

We go up and check the screws, the flashing, the gutters, the ridge and the sheets themselves, then repair what is causing the leak.

Report a leak
05

Roof carpentry

The wood under the roof: wall plate, trusses, rafters and purlins.

We set up the wood work for new roofs and repair or replace old wood during re-roofing.

06

Gutters and flashing

To carry rain water away from your walls and keep the edges of the roof closed.

Rain gutters with pipes, outlets, joiners, end caps, hangers and clips. On the roof: ridge cap, barge board, eaves angle, and flashing where the roof meets a wall. We can fit these with a new roof or on their own.

07

Cladding and ceilings

Aluminium fascia and ceiling cladding outside, PVC and POP ceilings inside.

Aluminium cladding on the fascia and under the eaves matches the roof and doesn't need painting.
