FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your question? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Where do you work?

We are based in Lagos and Ibadan and take on roofing projects across Nigeria and beyond.

Do you inspect the site before quoting?

Yes. Before we give a final quotation we measure the building and check the walls, wall plates and timber. If you are far from Lagos or Ibadan, you can start by sending photos or your building plan on WhatsApp.

How much will my roof cost?

It depends on the roof size and shape, the material and thickness, the timber needed, and the location. Use our roof calculator for the quantities, then send it to us for a price. The final price follows a site inspection.

How long does a roof take?

It depends on the roof size and shape, the weather, and whether the timber frame is ready. We give you a timeline with your quote.

Can you roof during the rainy season?

Yes, but we plan around the weather and protect the building when rain comes. If you can, book before the rains so the roof is on when you need it most.

How do payments work?

Payment is in stages linked to progress, so you never pay for work that hasn't been done. We agree the stages with you before work starts.

Can you work from my building plan?

Yes. Send a clear photo or PDF of the plan on WhatsApp. We will work out the roof quantities and confirm the measurements on site.

Do you repair roofs you didn't install?

Yes. We fix leaks and damaged roofs of all kinds, and we'll tell you honestly if a repair or a replacement makes more sense.
