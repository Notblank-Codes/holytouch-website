About us

About HolyTouch

We are an aluminium roofing company based at Academy, Olunde, Ibadan, and we also work out of Lagos.

Most of our customers have come to us through people we roofed for before: a neighbour, a relative, a friend at work. One of the jobs on this site is a good example: after we roofed a bungalow in blue longspan, the man next door called us to do his own house. This website is for the people who haven't met us yet.

We do new roofs, re-roofing, leak repairs, gutters and cladding. Sometimes we supply everything; sometimes the customer has already bought the sheets and we just fix them.

Distance is not a problem. We take jobs in other states, and outside Nigeria too.

What to expect from us

We measure first

We come to the site, take our own measurements and check the wood before we give a final price.

Itemised quotes
