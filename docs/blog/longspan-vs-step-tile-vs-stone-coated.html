Longspan vs step tile vs stone coated: which roofing is best in Nigeria?

An honest comparison of the three most common roofing types in Nigeria: how they look, cost, sound in the rain and hold up, and which one suits your building.

By HolyTouch Roofing · 21 November 2026 · 3 min read

Longspan is the most affordable and the easiest to keep leak-free. Step tile gives you a tiled look in aluminium for a bit more money. Stone coated costs the most, looks the most premium and is the quietest in the rain. For most houses, 0.55mm longspan or step tile gives the best value.

Walk through any new estate in Ibadan or Lagos and you'll see all three on different houses. Which one you should use comes down to money, the look you like, and how your roof is shaped.

Longspan

Longspan sheets are cut to the full length of the roof slope, so there are no joints from the bottom of the roof to the top. Fewer joints means fewer places for water to get in. It is the most common choice for houses, schools and warehouses, and it is the cheapest per square metre. See current longspan prices.

Choose longspan if you want a durable roof at the best price, or you're roofing a big or low-slope building.

Step tile

Step tile is aluminium pressed into steps, so from the ground it looks like a tiled roof. It costs a little more than longspan. The steps are where thin sheets can give trouble, so many people avoid 0.45mm in step tile and go for 0.55mm.

Choose step tile if you want a tiled look without the price and weight of stone coated tiles.

Stone coated tiles

Stone coated tiles are metal tiles covered with a layer of stone chips. Gerard is one of the brands people know. They look the most expensive, and the stone layer makes them much quieter in heavy rain. They are sold per piece rather than per metre, and they need more careful fixing because there are many small tiles.

Choose stone coated if looks and a quiet roof matter most and the budget allows.

What about zinc and asbestos?

Galvanised zinc is cheap but rusts and gets very hot. Old asbestos roofs are still common on older buildings in Ibadan. Asbestos dust is dangerous to breathe, so don't cut, drill or break asbestos sheets yourself. If you're replacing an old asbestos or zinc roof, talk to us about re-roofing.

Our advice

  • Decide on your budget first, then the look.
  • Whatever you choose, don't go too thin. 0.55mm is a good standard for houses.
  • A good roofer matters as much as the material. The best sheet badly fixed will still leak.
  • Put a ceiling in. It cuts the rain noise and the heat from any metal roof.

Other questions

Which roofing sheet is best in Nigeria?

For most houses, 0.55mm aluminium longspan or step tile gives the best mix of price, durability and low maintenance. Stone coated is best if you want a premium look and a quieter roof.

Is stone coated better than aluminium?

It's quieter and looks more premium, but it costs more and has many more joints. Aluminium longspan is lighter, cheaper and has the fewest joints.

Does aluminium roofing rust?

No, aluminium doesn't rust the way zinc does. The screws can rust, so use good quality fixings.
