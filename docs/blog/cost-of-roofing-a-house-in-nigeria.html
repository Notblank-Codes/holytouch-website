How much does it cost to roof a 3-bedroom bungalow in Nigeria?

Real 2026 figures for roofing a 2, 3 or 4-bedroom bungalow in Nigeria with aluminium longspan: sheets, accessories, wood and workmanship, from a roofing company in Ibadan and Lagos.

By HolyTouch Roofing · 21 November 2026 · 4 min read

Short answer: for a typical 3-bedroom bungalow (about 15m × 10m), the roofing materials in 0.55mm longspan come to roughly ₦3.4 million to ₦4 million, using our prices from August 2026. The wood work, workmanship and transport are on top of that. The exact figure depends on your roof shape, the sheet thickness and where the site is.

"How much will my roof cost?" is the first question almost everybody asks us, and it's a fair one. The roof is one of the biggest bills on any building, and prices in Nigeria have moved a lot in the last two years. Below we break down what goes into the cost, with real numbers from the quotations we give customers in Ibadan and Lagos.

What you are paying for

A roofing quotation is made up of four parts:

  1. Roofing sheets, priced by the running metre. This is usually the biggest single item.
  2. Accessories: ridge cap, barge board, fascia cladding, eaves angle, wall flashing, drive screws, seam bolts, rivets, silicone and flash band.
  3. Wood work: wall plate, tie beams, rafters, king posts, struts, purlins and fascia board.
  4. Workmanship and transport.

When you compare two quotations, check that both list all four parts. A quotation that looks cheap is often missing the accessories or the wood, which then show up later as "extra".

What makes the price go up or down

1. Sheet thickness

Going from 0.45mm to 0.55mm adds roughly 30% to the roofing materials. The thicker sheet is stronger, dents less and is quieter in the rain. We explain the difference in our guide to aluminium roofing sheet prices.

2. Roof shape

A hip roof (sloping on all four sides) uses more sheet than a gable roof on the same building, because the sheets are cut at an angle at the hips and some of the offcut is wasted. It also needs hip rafters and more ridge cap. Expect a hip roof to cost about 10–15% more than a gable.

3. Roof slope

The steeper the roof, the longer each sheet and rafter. A steeper roof sheds water faster, but you pay for the extra material.

4. Type of roofing

Longspan is usually the cheapest of the aluminium options. Step tile costs a bit more, and stone coated tiles cost more again. See longspan vs step tile vs stone coated.

5. Location and access

Transport, scaffolding and how easy it is to get materials onto the roof all affect the final price. On our recent jobs, workmanship and transport together ranged from about ₦175,000 on a smaller roof to about ₦800,000 on a larger one.

6. Re-roofing

Replacing an old roof costs more than roofing a new building, because the old sheets have to come off and some of the old wood usually needs changing.

How to keep the cost down without a bad roof

  • Get your wall plate level and your walls square before the roofers arrive. Fixing a bad wall top on the roof is expensive.
  • Keep the roof design simple. Every valley, dormer and change in level adds material, cutting and labour.
  • Don't drop the sheet thickness below what your roof needs. A thin sheet that leaks costs more in repairs than you saved.
  • Buy the materials yourself if you have a reliable supplier, and pay only for installation. We do installation only as well as full supply and fix.
  • Roof before the rainy season. Work goes faster and the building is protected sooner.

Questions people ask

How much is the roofing of a 3-bedroom flat in Nigeria in 2026?

For a 15m × 10m building with 0.55mm longspan, the roofing materials come to about ₦3.4M–₦4M at our August 2026 prices. Wood work, workmanship and transport are added on top.

Is it cheaper to buy the roofing sheets myself?

It can be, if you buy the right thickness and the right quantity from a trusted supplier. Many people overbuy or underbuy. If you do buy yourself, ask your roofer for the full list of sheets and accessories first.

Why do roofing quotations differ so much?

Usually because they are not quoting the same thing: a different sheet thickness, missing accessories, no wood work, or no transport. Ask for an itemised quotation so you can compare line by line.
