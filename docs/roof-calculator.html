What the calculator can and can't tell you

Roof area, sheet numbers and lengths, ridge, hip and gutter lengths are geometry, so they are reliable for simple gable, hip and flat roofs when the measurements are right.

Screws and timber are indicative. They depend on the truss design, spans, wall layout and timber sizes, which we confirm on site. L-shaped roofs, valleys and roofs on several levels need a proper take-off from your plan.

Before your final quote, we always check:

  1. Site measurements: walls are rarely perfectly square, and plans can differ from what was built.
  2. Structure: wall plates, beams and ring beam to carry the roof.
  3. Timber: type, treatment against termites and rot, and moisture.
  4. Wind, rain, drainage, gutter size and fixing pattern.
  5. For re-roofing: condition of the old timber, removal and leak sources.
  6. Access, scaffolding, transport distance and site security.
  7. Current material prices and labour.
