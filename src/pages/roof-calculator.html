Good to know

How close is the calculator?

For a simple gable, hip or flat roof, the roof size, sheets, ridge, barge board and fascia come out close, as long as the measurements you put in are right.

The wood count is only a guide, because it depends on how the trusses are set out on your building. For L-shaped roofs, valleys and roofs on more than one level, send us the plan.

Before we give you a final price we check:

  1. The real measurements on site. Walls are not always square, and buildings don't always follow the plan.
  2. The wall plate, beams and walls that will carry the roof.
  3. The wood: type, treatment against termites, and condition.
  4. Where water will run off, and the gutters.
  5. For re-roofing: the state of the old wood and where leaks are coming from.
  6. Access, scaffolding and transport to the site.
  7. Current market prices.
