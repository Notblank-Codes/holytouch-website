Thickness (gauge)

Aluminium roofing sheets are sold by thickness, for example 0.45 mm or 0.55 mm. Thicker sheets are stronger, dent less and are quieter in rain, but cost more. For most homes we recommend a mid-to-thick sheet. Ask us what suits your building.

Tip: when comparing quotes, check that both list the same thickness. A cheaper quote is often a thinner sheet.

Colours

Common colours include brick red, wine, blue, green, charcoal and black, depending on the material and what suppliers have in stock. Send us a picture of a roof colour you like and we will tell you what's available.

How long will it last?

That depends on the sheet thickness, how well it is fixed and flashed, the timber underneath and the local environment. A well-installed roof on sound timber lasts far longer than a cheap one rushed on. That's why we check the frame before we fit any sheets.
