0.45mm or 0.55mm?

This is the thickness of the aluminium sheet. 0.55mm is stronger, doesn't dent as easily and makes less noise when it rains. It also costs more. A lot of people use 0.55mm for houses.

When comparing prices from different roofers, check the thickness. A cheaper price is often for a thinner sheet.

Colours

Common colours are red, wine, blue, green, grey and black. What's available depends on the sheet type and on the market at the time. Send us a picture of a roof colour you like.

How long will it last?

That depends on the sheet thickness, the wood underneath and how well the roof is fixed and sealed. That's why we check the wood before we put sheets on it.
