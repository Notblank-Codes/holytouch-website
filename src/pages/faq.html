FAQ

Questions people ask us

If your question isn't here, send it to us on WhatsApp.

Where do you work?

Our bases are Academy, Olunde in Ibadan, and Lagos. We also travel for jobs in other states and outside Nigeria.

Can you fix materials I have already bought?

Yes. We do installation only. We will look at what you have bought and let you know if anything is missing before we start.

Do you come to see the building before quoting?

Yes. We measure the building and check the walls and the wood before giving a final price. If you are far away, you can start by sending your building plan or pictures on WhatsApp.

How much will my roof cost?

It depends on the size and shape of the roof, the type and thickness of the sheet, the wood work needed and where the site is. The roof calculator gives you a rough idea. Our quotation lists every item.

Which is better, 0.45mm or 0.55mm?

0.55mm is thicker. It is stronger, doesn't dent as easily and makes less noise in the rain, but it costs more. Many people use 0.55mm for houses. We will advise you.

How long does a roof take?

It depends on the size of the roof, the weather and whether the wood work is ready. We tell you the timing when we give you the quotation.

Can you roof in the rainy season?

Yes, we plan the work around the weather. If you can, start before the rains.

How do I pay?

Payment is in stages as the work goes on. We agree the stages with you before we start.

Can you work from my building plan?

Yes. Send a clear picture or PDF of the plan on WhatsApp and we will work out the quantities, then confirm the measurements on site.

Do you repair roofs you didn't fix?

Yes. We repair all kinds of roofs, and we will tell you honestly if a repair is enough or the roof needs changing.
