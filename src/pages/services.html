Roofing services

Everything a roof needs, from the timber frame to the last ridge cap. Tell us what you need and we will advise on the best option for your building and budget.

01

New roofing

For new buildings, from bungalows and duplexes to churches, schools and warehouses.

We install the three main roof types used in Nigeria:

  • Long-span aluminium: long single sheets cut to the length of your roof slope, so there are fewer joints for water to get through.
  • Step-tile aluminium: aluminium sheets pressed to look like tiles, for a more finished look.
  • Stone-coated tiles: steel tiles with a stone-chip finish, quieter in heavy rain.

See the materials guide to compare them.

02

Re-roofing and roof replacement

When an old roof is rusted, sagging or leaking in many places, replacing it costs less than repairing it again and again.

We remove the old roof and check every piece of timber. We replace what is rotten or termite-damaged, then fit the new roof. We also take the old sheets away.

03

Repairs and leak fixing

Most leaks start somewhere other than where the water drips.

We trace the leak to its source: loose or rusted screws, failed flashing, a blocked gutter, a damaged sheet or a badly finished ridge. Then we fix the cause properly.

04

Roof trusses and carpentry

A good roof needs a straight, strong frame under it.

We build the timber frame: wall plates, rafters or trusses, purlins and fascia. We size and space them for the roofing you choose, using treated timber where it matters.

05

Gutters, fascia, ridge caps and flashing

The finishing details that keep water off your walls and out of your ceiling.

We fit gutters and downpipes, fascia, ridge caps, and flashing at walls, chimneys and valleys. We can add them to a new roof, or fit them on their own to an existing roof.

06

Ceilings

PVC and POP ceilings to finish the inside once the roof is on.

Fitting the ceiling straight after the roof means one team, one schedule, and a finished building sooner.
