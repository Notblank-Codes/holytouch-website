New roofing

Supply and installation for new buildings: bungalows, duplexes, schools, shops and warehouses.

  • Longspan: one sheet runs from the edge of the roof to the top, so there are fewer joints.
  • Step tile: aluminium pressed in steps so it looks like tiles.
  • Stone coated: metal tiles with a stone finish (Gerard and similar). Quieter when it rains.

Most of our customers go for 0.45mm or 0.55mm. If you want something thicker or thinner, or a particular colour, tell us. The materials page explains the differences.

Re-roofing

For old roofs that are rusted, sagging or leaking in many places.

We remove the old sheets, check the wood and change any that is rotten or eaten by termites, then fix the new roof. The old sheets are taken away when we finish.

{{icon:whatsapp}} Ask about re-roofing

Signs it may be time to change the roof

  • Rust or holes across many sheets
  • Leaks in several rooms, not just one spot
  • The roof line sags between trusses
  • Wood that is soft, rotten or has termites

Leak repairs

Water rarely comes in where it drips. We find where it really gets in.

We go up and check the screws, the side laps, the ridge cap, the flashing and the gutters, then repair the cause. We repair roofs we didn't fix too.

Roof carpentry

The wood under the roof: wall plate, trusses, rafters and purlins.

We set up the wood work for new roofs, and repair or replace old wood during re-roofing. A straight, well-braced frame is what keeps the sheets flat and the roof free of leaks.

Gutters and flashing

So rain water goes away from your walls, and the edges of the roof are closed up.

Rain gutters with pipes, outlets, joiners, end caps, hangers and clips. On the roof: ridge cap, barge board, eaves angle, and flashing where the roof meets a wall. We fit these with a new roof or on their own.
