About us

A roofing company built on referrals

HolyTouch Roofing Ltd is an Ibadan roofing company. For years, most of our work has come from clients who recommended us to family, friends and colleagues. This website lets new clients see our work for themselves.

We specialise in aluminium roofing: long-span, step-tile and stone-coated roofs, re-roofing and repairs. We also build the timber frames and fit the gutters, flashing and ceilings that finish the job.
