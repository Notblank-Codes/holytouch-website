About HolyTouch

We are an aluminium roofing company based at Academy, Olunde, Ibadan, and we also work out of Lagos.

Most of our customers have come to us through people we roofed for before: a neighbour, a relative, a friend at work. This website is so that people who don't know us yet can see what we do and reach us directly.

Roofing is our work. We do new roofs, re-roofing, leak repairs, gutters and cladding, either supplying the materials ourselves or fixing materials the customer has already bought.

We are happy to travel for a job, whether it's in another state or outside Nigeria.
