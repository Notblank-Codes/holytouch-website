Short answer: most bungalow roofs in Nigeria are built from trusses about 1.2m apart. Each truss has a tie beam, two rafters, a king post and two struts. Purlins run across the rafters about 0.9m apart, and the wall plate sits on the walls. Count the length of each part, divide by 3.6m (12 ft), and round up.

Wood is the part of the roof people find hardest to estimate, and it's where a lot of money disappears on site. Here is the way roofers count it, so you can check a list before you go to the timber market.

Worked example: a 15m × 10m bungalow

Gable roof, 18° slope, 0.6m overhang, wood sold in 3.6m (12 ft) lengths.

1. Number of trusses

The roof is 16.2m long with the overhang. At 1.2m apart: 16.2 ÷ 1.2 = 13.5, so 14 spaces and 15 trusses.

2. Tie beams (2x6)

Each tie beam spans the 10m width, so it's made of joined lengths: 15 × 10m = 150m ÷ 3.6m = 42 lengths.

3. Rafters (3x4)

Each rafter is about 5.9m (the same as the sheet length), two per truss: 30 × 5.9m = 177m ÷ 3.6m = 50 lengths.

4. King posts (2x6)

The height from wall plate to ridge at 18° is about 1.6m, so two king posts come out of each 3.6m length: 15 ÷ 2 = 8 lengths.

5. Struts (3x4)

Two per truss, each about 2.6m long, one per length: 30 lengths.

6. Purlins (2x2)

The roof surface is about 191m². At 0.9m apart, that's about 212m of purlin ÷ 3.6m = 59 lengths.

7. Wall plate (3x4)

The wall plate goes on all the walls that carry the roof, inside walls included. That's often around twice the outside perimeter: 2 × 50m = 100m ÷ 3.6m = 28 lengths.

8. Fascia board (1x12)

Round the edges of the roof: about 56m ÷ 3.6m = 16 lengths.

Before allowing for offcuts and mistakes, that's around 230 lengths of wood. Add a margin, because some pieces will be crooked or cracked.

Tips before you buy wood

  • Buy seasoned, straight wood. Wet wood shrinks and twists after the sheets are on, and that loosens screws and causes leaks.
  • Treat the wood against termites, especially the wall plate and anything touching the blocks.
  • Check every length at the market. Reject pieces with large knots, splits or rot.
  • Keep the wood off the ground and covered until it's used.

Questions people ask

How many pieces of wood do I need to roof a 3-bedroom bungalow?

For a 15m × 10m gable roof, roughly 230 lengths of 12 ft wood across all sizes, before allowing for offcuts. A hip roof or a steeper slope needs more.

What size wood is used for roofing in Nigeria?

Commonly 2x6 for tie beams and king posts, 3x4 for rafters, struts and wall plate, 2x2 or 2x3 for purlins, and 1x12 for fascia board. Carpenters differ, so confirm with yours.

Is the ceiling wood included?

No. The noggins for the ceiling are counted separately with the ceiling work.
